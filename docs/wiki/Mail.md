# Mail

Add **Mailpit** to a space and point your app's SMTP at `127.0.0.1:<port>`; every email is caught and shown in **Mail** instead of being sent.

## Sending for real
**Mail → Relay** lets you forward chosen emails through a real SMTP server: presets for **Gmail** (smtp.gmail.com:587 with an app password) and **Outlook**, or any server. Nothing is forwarded automatically unless you turn on *relay all*. Relay passwords are stored privately per space.
